This course offered a **first approach to experimental research** by studying **physical and electronic systems**, showing different **experimental methodologies** and **safety protocols** as well as **scientific communication strategies**.

Students **reviewed the following topics**: impulse excitation technique, linear regression, measurement errors, Ohm's and Kirchhoff's laws as well as the functioning of capacitors, AC current and filters (high-pass, low-pass and band-pass). Students also **acquired lab equipment proficiency** by using power supply, function generator, multimeter, protoboard, caliper, and phyphox toolbox. **Lectures** focused on explaining the theoretical fundamentals of each phenomenon and applied examples, while the **assessments** consisted of **pre-lab quizzes** to ensure lab readiness, the development of **formal lab reports** following a standard structure (abstract, introduction, methodology, results, analysis, discussion, and conclusions), as well as **regular exams** that evaluated the laboratory methodology and strategies learned throughout the course.

As a **teaching assistant**, I was responsible for **guiding students in the use of electronics** during laboratory sessions, as well as **grading the pre-lab quiz** at the start of each lab session to assess their preparation. Also, I took the initiative to **make and share cheat sheets for each topic** so that students could access the relevant contents during their laboratory sessions :) (and for the record, I've seen students using them almost 2 years after!!)

* **Lecturer Professor:** [Diego Guzmán Silva](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=l6hSvqEAAAAJ&hl=en), Physics PhD of the University of Rostock and Professor at the University of Chile. He works on theoretical and experimental light propagation studies, and also has worked at the [Millennium Institute for Research in Optics (MIRO)](https://www.miroptics.cl/) as well as at the [Amazing Quantum Laboratory](https://www.amazingquantum.com/). (Last updated: July, 2026).
* **Original name of the course:** "Métodos Experimentales".
* **Period:** December/2023 - January/2024 (summer term).
