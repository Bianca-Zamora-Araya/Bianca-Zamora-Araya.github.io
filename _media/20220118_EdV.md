During the COVID pandemic in Chile and while I was a high school student (2020-2021), I participated in several Escuela de Verano (EdV) courses. I was motivated by my curiosity to learn new topics, and because these experiences would allow me to explore my vocational interests. During my last course, called "Galaxies and the Distant Universe" (Galaxias y Universo Lejano), I was asked to answer some questions about my experience, and I happily agreed! :)
